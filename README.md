## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence (AI) can support social media marketing for a small business.

**Cake Corner** is a fictional cake shop created for this student capstone project. The project uses AI-assisted planning and content creation to prepare a 30-day social media calendar, draft captions, design promotional posters, and plan a short promotional Reel.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Create a 30-day social media content calendar.
- Draft 12 social media captions with hashtags.
- Prepare six matching promotional poster designs.
- Plan a short promotional Reel.
- Demonstrate a simple Google Sheets automation workflow.
- Compare AI-assisted work with a manual workflow.
- Review AI-generated content for accuracy, originality, and responsible use.

## 🚀 How to Use This Project

1. Open the Excel or CSV file in Google Sheets.
2. Review the 30-day themes, post types, content ideas, and hashtags.
3. Use the caption drafts as starting points and edit them for the intended audience.
4. Open the six poster images and use Canva to customise the designs.
5. Follow the Reel storyboard to create a short video in CapCut.
6. Follow the automation guide to move rows marked `READY` into a `Draft Queue` sheet.
7. Record your actual time for manual and AI-assisted work, then update the comparison.

## ⚙️ Automation

The project includes a Google Apps Script guide. It demonstrates how a row marked `READY` in the Google Sheets calendar can be copied to a `Draft Queue` sheet and marked `COPIED`.

The automation is for a student demonstration only. It does not automatically publish posts or send messages.

## 📊 AI vs Manual Workflow

A comparison template is included in `content/ai_vs_manual_comparison.txt`.

The included example times are **illustrative estimates, not measured results**. For your final submission, time both workflows yourself and replace the estimates with your actual observations. Include planning, editing, fact-checking, and review time.

## ✅ Responsible AI

- Cake Corner is a fictional business concept.
- Do not present sample captions or designs as real customer feedback.
- Verify product details and any food-safety advice before public use.
- Use original or properly licensed images, video, and music.
- Check spelling, cultural sensitivity, and accessibility.
- Do not include private customer information.
- Review all AI-generated content before publishing.

## 📌 Project Outcome

The project demonstrates how AI can assist a small business with content planning, caption drafting, visual content preparation, short-video planning, and basic workflow organisation. Human review remains important for quality and accuracy.
