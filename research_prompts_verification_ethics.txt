RESEARCH AND PROJECT ASSUMPTIONS
Business: Cake Corner (fictional)
Audience: Families, students, and working adults planning birthdays and small celebrations.
Audience needs assumed for this classroom project: clear cake inspiration, flavour ideas, easy-to-understand planning tips, and attractive visuals.
Content goals: build awareness, encourage harmless interaction (polls/comments), and show a consistent brand style.

This is a fictional concept, not a market-research claim about a real bakery. No customer survey or competitor analysis has been conducted. If the assignment requires primary research, conduct a short voluntary survey and report the actual responses without collecting sensitive personal information.

PROMPT ENGINEERING EXAMPLES
1. Calendar prompt:
“Act as a social media content planner. Create a 30-day calendar for a fictional small cake shop named Cake Corner. Audience: families, students and working adults. Include day, theme, post type, content idea and suitable hashtags. Use a warm, friendly tone. Do not invent prices, addresses, customer reviews or business claims.”

2. Caption prompt:
“Write one short Instagram caption for a fictional cake shop. Topic: cupcake spotlight. Use a cheerful but natural tone, one simple engagement question, and relevant hashtags. Do not claim products are available for purchase.”

3. Visual prompt:
“Create a clean pastel bakery poster for a fictional brand called Cake Corner. Topic: birthday cake inspiration. Use cream and pastel pink, readable text, generous spacing, and no phone number, address, price or fake customer testimonial.”

4. Verification prompt:
“Review this draft for unsupported factual claims, unclear wording, cultural sensitivity, spelling, and copyright risks. List concerns and suggest safe revisions. Do not assume a claim is true.”

VERIFICATION / ERROR LOG
Potential AI issue | How to check or correct
Invented product availability | Confirm with the business owner; for this fictional project, describe items as inspiration only
Incorrect food storage advice | Check authoritative food-safety guidance before publishing; otherwise use cautious wording
Fake prices, reviews, address or contact details | Remove them; none are supplied in this project
Cultural or festival mismatch | Check dates, local context and respectful wording before use
Copyrighted image or music | Use original work or assets with a clear licence; keep a record of the licence
Spelling or awkward text in poster | Proofread every visual at phone-screen size

RESPONSIBLE AI CHECKLIST
- Clearly identify Cake Corner as fictional in the student submission.
- Do not invent customer testimonials, sales results, prices, contact details or product claims.
- Use original visuals or properly licensed assets.
- Verify food-related claims before public posting.
- Review language for cultural sensitivity and accessibility.
- Do not upload private customer information.
- Do not automate publishing or direct messages to real people without consent.
- Keep a record of prompts, edits and errors.
